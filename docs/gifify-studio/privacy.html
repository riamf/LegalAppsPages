Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

Gifify Studio ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application.

1. Information We Collect

Local Data: Gifify Studio processes video files and images locally on your device for editing and GIF generation. We do not upload your personal media to our servers.

In-App Purchases: To manage subscriptions and lifetime unlocks, we use RevenueCat. This service may collect information related to your Apple ID and purchase history to verify your "Pro" status. This data is handled by Apple and RevenueCat in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

2. How We Use Information

We use the minimal amount of information necessary to provide our services, specifically:

  • Processing media locally for video editing and GIF creation.
  • Verifying in-app purchases via RevenueCat to unlock premium features.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information or media content to third parties. We only share data with service providers necessary for app functionality:

  • Apple: For processing in-app purchases and App Store services.
  • RevenueCat: To manage subscription states and entitlement verification.

4. Data Retention

Since most processing happens on your device, we do not store your videos or GIFs on our servers. Any files you export are stored locally on your device according to your iOS system settings.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the integrity of the app, but please note that no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Gifify Studio does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover such data has been collected, we will take steps to delete it immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the support options provided in the app.
